# Author-review requirements

This checklist records the 2 October 2026 review of the COR draft. It governs
presentation, not changes to frozen experimental observations or policy.

1. Define class-aware in the abstract and method, consistently with the title.
2. Explain partial starting values and solver completion in readable academic prose.
3. Cite MILPBench formally at first mention and preserve source provenance.
4. Use no bold emphasis in article prose or figure/table labels.
5. State a clear research question about instances not used for fitting.
6. Explain the root-LP comparator's equal support size and positive count.
7. Describe contributions positively without defensive theoretical disclaimers.
8. Consolidate limitations in final considerations; never fabricate data deposition.
9. Introduce each figure before display and explain its interpretation afterward.
10. Describe original-instance training as the completed method; place augmentation
    assessment among future studies.
11. Define all reported metrics before Results, using referenced, reproducible
    conventions, including the distinction between AP and trapezoidal PR-AUC.
12. Introduce and interpret every table; explain right censoring before Results.
13. Use grayscale, distinguishable styles, clear units, and per-instance gap labels.
14. Start every section with narrative text rather than a display.
15. Keep captions short; provide abbreviations and timing scope in notes. Do not add repetitive "Source" sentences after figures or tables; provenance remains in citations, methods, and hash manifests.
16. Discuss findings relative to the literature and the research question; avoid
    repeating limitations throughout Discussion.
17. End with Final considerations, limitations and future work: objective and
    achievement, limitations, then future opportunities.

Complete paired measurements, regressions, and influence diagnostics remain in
the main result tables and figures. Relocation of historical negative experiments
does not authorize selective omission. No general speedup or confirmatory
significance is inferred from this development study.

The reserved/public Zenodo DOI must be independently verified before insertion.
The official reservation workflow is described at
<https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/describe-records/reserve-doi/>.
A draft reservation does not establish public dataset availability. Archive
planning must retain unsuccessful attempts and the rights of upstream inputs.
