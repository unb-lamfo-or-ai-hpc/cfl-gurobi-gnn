# Elsevier typesetting provenance

The unmodified `elsarticle.cls` (version 3.5, 9 January 2026) and
`elsarticle-harv.bst` are distributed by Elsevier through CTAN/TeX Live.
Their source headers and LPPL notices are preserved. They are not relicensed
under MIT. Distribution source and SHA256 digests are recorded in
`elsevier.json`.

The LaTeX Project Public License is available at
<https://www.latex-project.org/lppl/>. Corresponding package source is
available at <https://ctan.org/pkg/elsarticle>. The project-written Quarto
adapter in `_extensions/elsevier/template.tex` is MIT licensed and does not
modify the upstream class or bibliography style.

The earlier quarto-sbc extension and its original provenance remain for
historical reproducibility. They do not typeset the current article.
