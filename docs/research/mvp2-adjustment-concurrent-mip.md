# MVP 2.0 adjustment sprint: paired warm starts and concurrent MIP

## Why a separate campaign

The one-hour study used one physical solver thread and cannot estimate the effect of concurrent MIP. A single available thread permits at most one search, so the absent effective parameter in the archive does not invalidate the one-thread contrast. The proposed campaign extends the design and retains the earlier results as separate evidence.

The primary contrast is B versus G, which measures the effect of supplying the complete learned partial Start. Retain R as a secondary control. R takes the same assignment and positive counts as G but ranks the root-relaxation values, supplying its own indices. G versus R compares candidate construction under matched counts. It cannot identify the contribution of one neural feature or represent a standalone LP heuristic. Preparation for R includes the upstream dependency on G.

## Fixed design

- Easy: E0, E3, E6, E14, E27, E29. Medium: M0, M4, M5, M6, M7, M9, M12, M13, M20, M26. Hard: H0, H5, H10, H15, H20, H25. E is the publication alias for the archived F prefix. Use the original `CFL_*_instance_*` IDs and hashes to bind models.
- Preserve B/M0, R/M1, G/M2, the fixed learning split and the frozen CPU checkpoint. No retraining or tuning on the evaluation cohort. Hard cases probe transfer beyond the training difficulty range. Prior PaScal work is provenance to check for model identity and feasibility of instrumentation, without equating its clocks, wrapper or solutions with this experiment.
- Pair B/R/G within each instance, solver seed (42, 43, 44) and profile. Rotate order across seeds. Every arm reads a fresh model and enforces MINIMIZE, with unchanged bounds and domains, 3,600 s solver limit, 0.01% target and the same inherited tolerance map. Capture root/graph/score/Starts once per instance, outside evaluation, then bind the same bytes across the matrix. An abstention and a failed or unavailable preparation have distinct dispositions.
- Profiles (Threads, ConcurrentMIP): (1,1), (4,1), (4,2), (8,1), (8,2). At four and eight threads, contrast one search with two at fixed total allocation. Within conventional MIP, compare thread caps with fixed instance/seed/method and comparable observed termination. At ConcurrentMIP=2, each racer receives approximately half the total threads. These are separate within-policy scaling and between-policy performance questions.
- Gurobi concurrent MIP is nondeterministic even with the same seed. The three seeds are blocked search perturbations, not 66 independent instances or repeatability replicates. [Gurobi parameter reference](https://docs.gurobi.com/projects/optimizer/en/current/reference/parameters.html#concurrentmip).
- Gurobi reports callback progress from only the first concurrent instance. Use terminal incumbent, bound, gap, solver status and Runtime for all profiles. Do not use callback-derived times, node counts or first-racer Start messages to characterize the winning concurrent search. Use budgeted attainment with recorded runtime and right-censor genuine timeouts. Give overshoot, infeasibility, numerical restrictions, missing preparation and infrastructure failures separate dispositions. [Gurobi concurrent callbacks](https://docs.gurobi.com/projects/optimizer/en/current/features/concurrent.html#callbacks).
- Summarize seed-paired contrasts within each instance and profile before stratifying by difficulty. Report primal and dual outcomes separately, matched quality time ratios where eligible, fixed-budget attainment, missingness and resource costs. Continue the M4/M12 exclusions for certified time comparisons unless an independent new audit qualifies them. E/M/H assess scale-up across problem instances, without fixing workload per core as a weak-scaling study.

## Implementation and execution gates

1. Offline: `python -B scripts/evidence/mvp2_concurrent_mip.py matrix`. It enumerates 330 blocks and 990 proposed solves. The solve ceiling is 4,950 physical-core-hours. Reservations cap at 6,600 physical-core-hours, with a four-hour wall limit and 64 GiB per block. The three arrays are chained, with at most four concurrent blocks, 32 physical cores and 256 GiB peak. Preparation is additional. Scheduler accounting, memory, license capacity, sibling placement, I/O and node availability must be checked on the actual site. Equal requested caps are not measured utilization.
2. Confirm the six hard model bytes against the original MILPBench identity and any available PaScal records. Qualify a bounded preparation and hardware pilot on hard inputs before full budget review. Hard memory, storage, root-vector availability and graph inference remain unverified. Existing E/M preparations may be reused only after byte and lineage checks. Root, graph, scores and Starts require individual seals. No fallback starts or silently dropped parents.
3. Prepare a private bindings JSON keyed by E/M/H alias. Each row contains parent, checkpoint_sha256, model and model_sha256, starts and private_starts_sha256, reviewed_preparation and reviewed_preparation_sha256, M1_assignment_sha256, M2_assignment_sha256, binary_variables, selected_support, positive_assignments and abstained. The reviewed preparation is a receipt agreeing on those IDs and hashes and recording root_sha256, graph_sha256 and prediction_sha256. Verify those artifacts and the preparation contract before author review of the receipt. Keep private paths, raw logs and models off Git.
4. On dgx-dasci, activate tfm_env, install the reviewed clean source separately, then use `freeze STAGE INPUTS_JSON` under the evidence data root. Inspect the printed plan digest and budget. Only after explicit approval record `authorization.json` in that stage with `plan_sha256`, the exact `budget` object, `execution_approved: true` and the decision reference.
5. Use `queue-held STAGE PLAN_SHA256 ACCOUNT PARTITION`. It creates three held arrays, with durable submission claims and no retry. Review all returned Slurm allocations, limits, affinities, dependencies and array cardinalities before author CLI release. The worker refuses a mismatched source, plan, approval, allocation or array identity. Partial submission stays held for reconciliation. Never repeat submission, cancel another campaign, or release after an ambiguous receipt.
6. Reconcile all planned cells with Slurm and the private per-cell result or intent files. Seal the returns and qualify metrics before promoting claims. Keep the campaign separate from the one-hour study. The next PR will integrate qualified results and record the final MVP 2.0 limits.

The matrix and worker are review code. Real hard preparation, site/solver admission and full end-to-end collection validation precede an execution decision.
