Work units are printed log observations, not reconstructed terminal
attributes. G uses about50.41times less recorded work than B and46.44times

time and the65–68s outside Runtime cannot explain the hours. This provides
direct evidence of substantially different solver effort, not merely a

The much better loaded incumbent precedes G's stronger presolve reductions.
An incumbent can support objective-based reductions and alter subsequent
search. The observations are consistent with this mechanism, but there is
no controlled ablation isolating which presolve reductions, cuts or branch
decisions account for the advantage. G's root objective is printed as
0.8767402, versus0.8763416 for B/R; these are different processed relaxations,
not evidence that a worse local solution trapped a global tree.

The logs explicitly name a **deterministic concurrent LP optimizer**.
At four threads primal simplex wins in all three cases; B at two threads
instead reports dual simplex. Do not infer nondeterministic concurrency
merely from `Method=-1`. Deterministic search and numerical exactness are
different properties. Likewise, post-Start log fingerprints differ, while
the original-byte hash and mathematical signatures agree. Fingerprints
include optimization-relevant attributes, as described by
[Gurobi](https://docs.gurobi.com/projects/optimizer/en/current/reference/attributes/model.html#fingerprint);
they are not used alone to infer a different feasible region.

The final logs confirm equal within-run objective/bound values. No
allowlisted numerical-trouble, unscaled-violation or large-matrix-range
flag appears. The partial-start completion warning is informational and
does not diagnose numerical trouble. Lack of a warning does not negate
the independently measured tolerance-sensitive feasibility.
