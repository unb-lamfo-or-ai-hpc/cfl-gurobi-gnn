# Editorial scope

The manuscript presents an original-instance graph-learning study, not a completed neural solver benchmark. Its results cover source-label admission, unsuccessful repair attempts, graph statistics and PCA, 100-epoch training, validation-based selection and eight-parent held-out evaluation.

The discussion separates predictive ranking from calibration and optimization usefulness. The strict 42-parent objective failed; the revised 39-parent cohort is outcome-conditioned. The study has one seed, no hard instances and no current-cohort four-arm or native-guidance comparison.

Future work includes paired augmentation and label-source studies, full-model solver interventions, calibration, multiple-seed replication and the deferred 90-parent extension.

The text is academic English. The body limit is 20 pages including front matter and declarations, with references excluded. Journal-specific typesetting and mandatory submission declarations must be reconciled with the selected journal; the current presentation uses the pinned SBC template.
