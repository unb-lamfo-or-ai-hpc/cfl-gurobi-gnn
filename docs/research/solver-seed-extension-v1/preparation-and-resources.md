# Preparation and resource proposal for the solver-seed extension

This document makes the next execution package reviewable without running it.
The six original-file identities are verified. Their roots, graphs, scores and
partial starts have not been generated. The current CPU12 h campaign and its
installed source remain unchanged.

## What can be reused

[Historical artifact bindings](historical-artifact-bindings.json) join the
ten original parents' root receipt (job3520) to the prospective guidance
receipt (job3521). Each chain retains the input, root, ordered-vector,
mathematical-signature, graph, checkpoint, score and R/G assignment hashes.
This is a reconciliation of existing public receipts, not another private-byte
audit or permission to repeat those roots or forwards. The 90 archived
one-thread observations remain subject to their metric-specific restrictions.

The [source registry](source-bindings.json) identifies the reference files and
their LF-normalized hashes. It records mechanisms that can be reused; it is
not a complete dependency closure or an installed PR94 executor. The existing
root and guidance operators are bound to PR86 stages, indices and manifests.
They must not be invoked for six new parents by editing their historical
constants, pointing them at PR89, or bypassing their admission checks.

## Six-parent preparation chain

The [preparation contract](preparation-contract.json) and
[pending artifact ledger](new-artifact-bindings.json) retain the six input
hashes and define the following sequence.

1. Load the identified original through the native Gurobi adapter, preserving
   every coefficient, row, bound and variable domain. Apply the accepted
   MINIMIZE correction. Record the mathematical signature and original variable
   order before optimization.
2. Capture the first OPTIMAL MIPNODE observation at node count zero and
   terminate that preparation call. Use the historical root policy:
   Threads=1, Seed=42, Presolve=0, NodeLimit=1, TimeLimit=600 and SoftMemLimit=48
   decimal GB. No model.relax(), zero vector or different root method replaces
   a missing observation. Capture/serialization failures stop dependent work;
   they do not permit retries.
3. Build inference features with the existing label-free Gurobi interface and
   production encoder. The historical guidance wrapper requires a reference
   solution and ten test-parent indices; it cannot be used unchanged here.
   The label-free interface accepts the model/root, not a target solution.
   It removes temporary encoder targets and outcome metadata before returning
   the graph. No labels are generated, inspected or fitted.
4. Verify names, binary order, edge directions and the seven variable, five
   constraint and one edge channels. Variable channel 6 must encode the same
   ordered root values. The current encoder supplies the feature transforms;
   the checkpoint supplies its trained normalization state. Neither is refitted.
   The adapter now has synthetic parity tests against the production feature
   encoder and runtime serialized round-trip checks. Passing synthetic CI does
   not certify graphs that have not yet been generated on the HPC.
5. Load the original CPU-study checkpoint once on one V100 GPU, preserve its
   architecture, validation-selected threshold and evaluation settings, and
   perform at most one forward per parent, sequentially. Preserve the
   historical module-prefix handling. There is no CPU fallback, training,
   threshold search or repeated forward to select a favorable output.
6. Apply the historical class-aware G rule. Build R from the same root, matching
   G's actual support and positive count. Preserve abstention. Freeze one pair
   of R/G assignments per parent for solver seeds 42/43/44. Unselected variables
   remain undefined Start entries; none is fixed.

Root, graph, score and Start hashes in the six-parent ledger are intentionally
null. They become bindings only after actual generation and independent
receipt review. The historical ten-parent hashes must never fill those slots.

The learning corpus remains 54 parents, with 34/10/10 train/validation/test
partitions. Canonical roles and prior exposure are retained even when a parent
is used for this optimization extension. This is not a new untouched
generalization cohort or a neural training-seed ablation.

## A complete conditional reservation proposal

[reservation-budget.json](reservation-budget.json) is generated by the
offline planner. It assumes all 90 historical observations can be retained for
their admitted metrics, 54 new evaluation calls, and at most six new roots.
A failed reuse decision does not silently expand this budget.

| Stage | Jobs | Maximum per job | Physical cores | RAM per job | GPUs | Solver calls |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Six roots, sequential children |1|2 h|1|64 GiB|0|6|
| Features, frozen inference and R/G assembly |1|1 h|4|64 GiB|1 V100 32 GB|0|
| Parent/seed evaluation groups, three arms each |18|4 h|1|64 GiB|0|54|

Only one job is planned to execute at a time. The evaluation group order and
its three methods are copied from planned-cells.csv rather than sorted or
randomized again. Grouping preserves distinct children, fresh solver models,
durable call intentions and individual receipts; it is not a common solver
session or reuse of a previous arm's incumbent.

The resulting ceilings are:

- 55 optimizer-hours: 54 evaluation hours plus one hour for six bounded roots;
- 75 sequential reserved job-hours: 2+1+18×4;
- 78 physical-core-hours: 2×1+1×4+18×4×1;
- 1 reserved GPU-hour and 4800 GiB-hours of reserved host memory;
- 20 jobs, 60 optimization calls, at most six forwards and zero training calls.

These are different accounting boundaries and are not added as interchangeable
costs. A two-logical-CPUs-per-physical-core allocation would report up to 156
AllocCPU-hours under this reservation scenario; that is not a verified site
billing rule. Actual AllocTRES, affinity and physical core identities must be
recorded, as demonstrated by the historical guidance job's four physical
cores and eight AllocCPUS.

The root child deadline is 1140 s, leaving 360 s outside six children in a 2 h job.
The guidance child deadline is 3540 s. Evaluation children retain 3780 s each,
leaving 3060 s outside three children in a 4 h job. Those margins cover bounded
orchestration, auditing and export; they do not enlarge a 600 s root or 3600 s
evaluation TimeLimit. A deadline or resource failure preserves partial evidence
and stops continuation. It does not authorize an automatic extension or retry.

Use the existing memory policy: 48 decimal GB solver SoftMemLimit, sampled
interruption at 56 GiB, job-scoped 64 GiB kernel limit before model reading, and
failure on lost memory observation. These limits apply to process/job scopes
rather than certifying every Gurobi allocation. The GPU proposal retains the
historical V100 32 GB class; observed device/availability must be bound before
release. Its one-hour reservation covers feature preparation and assembly too,
not only CUDA kernel time.

The private working-storage allowance is proposed as 64 GiB, with at least 80 GiB
available at admission. This is a finite planning allowance, not a reserved
filesystem quota or an installed storage guard. The executor still needs
bounded artifacts/log exports and a stop-without-cleanup policy when the
storage allowance cannot be maintained. Originals are read in place; no home
captures or cloud/blob temporary storage are planned.

Queue time and human review are outside the 75 h ceiling. Actual host/GPU
utilization, energy and financial prices are unknown and are not inferred
from reservations. The earlier 125.837 s read-only preflight is an observed,
separate diagnostic cost, not a forecast for roots or inference.

## How preparation costs will be interpreted

Count root/features/inference once per parent in the physical campaign ledger,
plus the two Start assemblies. Do not double-count the preparation by summing
R and G counterfactual costs. R depends on the G assembly and actual support;
its standalone cost includes that dependency. For a within-method amortized
scenario, there are three solver-seed uses of the frozen preparation. Keep
shared model loading, serialization, process and job overhead explicit.

CUDA forward time is nested within inference time; neither is added twice.
Solver Runtime, supervised child time, batch time and Slurm elapsed have
different scopes. Missing costs remain unknown. No new end-to-end speedup
can be claimed from these proposed reservations.

## What remains before any HPC execution

The metadata planner has no solver, GPU or scheduler interface. It validates
source hashes, input/cell identities and unreleased flags, then emits JSON.
Offline tests cover adulterated bytes, changed checkpoint/model
hashes, incomplete returns, duplicate methods, unsafe source references and
attempted release. Local style checks use the installed Ruff 0.16.6; the
repository CI uses its pinned 0.16.8 and remains a separate final-head check.

The implemented isolated PR94 adapter covers source/dependency hashes,
checkpoint/private paths, feature/round-trip checks, one-time submission claims,
qualified memory supervision, affinity/storage gates and bounded collectors.
The first CLI binds the actual installation without scientific calls; later
root and guidance commands require their own reviewed plan/return hashes. Planning approval persists;
no renewed general planning approval is requested. No execution, merge,
publication or PDF generation is granted by this resource proposal.
