# Journal manuscript plan

The author-approved ceiling is **20 pages excluding references**. Front matter,
tables, figures, captions and declarations count towards the ceiling. This is an
internal writing budget, not a verified common limit of the candidate journals.
No content is added solely to fill the budget.

| Content | Maximum planned pages |
| --- | ---: |
| Title, author information and abstract | 1 |
| Introduction and research questions | 2 |
| Related work | 2 |
| Mathematical formulation and augmentation | 2 |
| Graph representation and GNN implementation | 2 |
| Experimental design and reproducibility | 3 |
| Accepted empirical results, tables and figures | 5 |
| Discussion, limitations and conclusion | 2 |
| Declarations | 1 |
| Total excluding references | 20 |

The pending results allocation covers collection statistics, graph statistics
and dimensionality reduction, a shared training/validation loss plot over 100
epochs, held-out predictive quality, and paired MIP-gap/time outcomes. Only
accepted, provenance-bound outputs may populate it. Extended diagnostics and
reproducibility receipts belong in reviewed supplementary material, not in an
uncontrolled appendix that hides additional main-text pages.

These are the authors' candidates, not an acceptance prediction or a selected
journal. Retain the supplied SBC working template for now. Once a venue is
chosen, check its current author guide, article type, length convention,
anonymization, citation style, highlights, supplementary files and declaration
requirements; then adapt the presentation without rewriting the scientific
source. The three ScienceDirect author-guide requests returned HTTP403 during
the 12 September 2026 check, so no journal-specific page rule is certified here.

The user-provided generative-AI declaration is reproduced verbatim immediately
before References in HTML and PDF. Model/tool names are author-provided usage
disclosures, not independently verified product identifiers. Authors must verify
that the declaration remains accurate for the submitted version.

The [Elsevier generative-AI policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals)
was consulted. Disclosure does not replace compliance with the separate figure,
artwork and research-method policies. Planned numerical figures must remain
traceable to measured data and plotting code; do not synthesize evidence or
substitute AI-generated illustrations for scientific results. Funding, CRediT
contributions, competing interests and any venue-specific declarations remain
editorial tasks; they are not inferred from author order.
